Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Application Purpose

This application uses rclone to access my personal Google Drive account for file transfers and backups.

Data Access and Usage

Google Drive data is accessed solely for user-authorized personal file management.

Data Storage

Downloaded files are stored on storage devices selected by the user. OAuth credentials are stored locally by rclone.

Data Sharing

The application does not intentionally share Google Drive contents with third parties.

Data Deletion

Users may delete downloaded files and revoke Google Drive access through their Google Account.
